The whole loop

Role posted to recruiter paid, in six steps.

I

Price the role

Set a fee, a guarantee window, and a salary band — live in minutes. No retainer, no lock-in contract.

Under 5 minutes to post
II

Reach the network

The role goes out to a thousand recruiters who already work your niche — not a black hole on a job board.

1,000 specialised recruiters
III

Match on requirements

Every candidate arrives with a scorecard and the reasoning behind it — a case to read, not a keyword match to guess at.

Reasoned, not keyword-matched
IV

Verify the submission

Client and recruiter both confirm the submission before it counts — so nobody argues later about who found who.

Dual sign-off
V

Move the pipeline

One pipeline, one truth. Client and recruiter watch the same stage update live — nobody's writing a status email.

Shared, real-time stages
VI

Pay on outcome

Payment triggers the moment the hire is confirmed. Seven figures moved through the system, and nobody had to chase an invoice.

Seven figures paid out
